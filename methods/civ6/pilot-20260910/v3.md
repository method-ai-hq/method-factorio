# Method v3: bring sustainable science forward

Retain the original capital; found two additional cities and grow each to population ≥3. Sustain science ≥20, treasury and net gold income ≥0, and food surplus ≥0 in all three cities for five complete rounds. Limits: 35 completed player turns, 1200 seconds, 1500 requests including reads/rejections. Optimize success, completed turns, requests, then playing time.

Use only `from game import act, observe` and the documented API. Make live decisions from observations; do not replay trial actions. Observe once, request map and relevant choices, then reuse returned state. Keep a compact ledger: completed turns, reported hold, populations/growth, food, production/completion estimates, science, gold/income, and next bottleneck. Use observed IDs, coordinates, purchase prices and production `turns`; `base_cost` is unscaled. Map yields: food, production, gold, science, culture, faith.

Start a Settler, available Writing, and an economic civic promptly. Normally produce both Settlers in the capital; deviate only for an earlier estimated qualification. Scout two nearby legal freshwater sites together, checking spacing and routes. Rank sites by arrival plus growth and useful Campus completion, preserving food/production tiles. Stop scouting when routes/sites suffice; fortify unneeded military units. Check actual arrival and remaining movement before founding; verify the city exists before assigning production.

Use initial Builder charges on soon-worked tiles that shorten Settler production, slow growth, or Campus completion. Check improvements at the unit's current tile. Compare capital improvements against supporting the slower new city, including travel. Start useful Campuses while new cities grow. Enable food focus only if absent; it toggles. At population 3, compare continued food focus with production/default allocation and science focus using observed yields and completion estimates. Preserve nonnegative food; retain extra growth only when it advances readiness or protects the hold.

After each completed turn, forecast the latest of population, science, and economy readiness, allowing five full qualifying rounds within the limit. Recalculate when builds, growth, or yields change. Treat science as a numerical shortfall: estimate each city's contribution at the proposed qualification date and through the hold. If the plan still falls below 20, change it now rather than waiting for scheduled builds.

Compare the earliest sustainable combination of Campuses, Libraries, purchases, available Campus projects, and science focus. Do not require three Libraries. Reserve gold for the purchase that advances qualification most, checking affordability and maintenance. Compare purchasing a slow Library with finishing an almost-complete one before spending. When a Library finishes or is purchased, immediately compare a Campus project against the next build. A granary, extra Builder, or other investment must improve the limiting date; surplus growth alone does not justify delaying science. If the youngest city's Campus is limiting, compare accelerating its production against supplying the missing science in established cities. Use current choices or targeted public rules to resolve material unknowns; never assume project yields, policy effects, or harvest returns.

Select available research/civics serving the forecast and compatible economic policies, favoring free changes. Avoid optional research switching and unrelated infrastructure. Inspect actual effects: `submitted` alone proves nothing. Refresh choices after relevant availability changes or rejection; do not repeat unavailable operations unchanged. Keep reasoning to state changes and the next decision; resolve returned turn blockers sequentially.

During the hold, verify every condition at each completed round. If required science depends on projects, anticipate completion and replace them when available; do not count temporary peaks as permanent output. Track reported hold, not calendar labels. Stop on explicit success or verified hold ≥5 with conditions intact; use `finish` only if still open. Never retry uncertain connection results. Stop on infrastructure failure and report it separately from gameplay failure.
