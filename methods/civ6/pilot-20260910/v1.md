# Method v1: expansion, growth, science, then hold

Use live observations to choose actions; this is a planning policy, not a script. Retain the original capital, found two additional cities, grow each to population 3, and sustain science ≥20, treasury and net gold income ≥0, and food surplus ≥0 in all three cities for five complete rounds. Finish within 35 completed player turns, 1200 seconds, and 1500 total requests. Aim to qualify by completed turn 30 or earlier, leaving five full rounds. The earlier 33/35-turn pilot successes indicate a tight target, not guaranteed reliability.

**Start and scout.** Import only `act` and `observe` from `game`. Observe once, request the revealed map and relevant choices, then start useful production, research, civic, and movement immediately. Send Scout and Warrior along different nearby routes to identify two settlement sites; no enemies means escorts and military production are unnecessary. Use actual observed IDs and coordinates throughout.

**Settle early.** Prefer reachable, legal freshwater sites with several immediately workable food tiles, enough production for a Campus and Library, and useful Campus adjacency. Favor short travel and rapid growth over distant ideal adjacency. Avoid settling on the only strong food tile or committing districts to essential growth tiles. Plan both sites together so spacing is legal. Select tiles using the documented food/production/gold/science/culture/faith yield order. Found promptly and verify founding succeeded.

The capital normally builds two Settlers early. Compare current production estimates against research timing: a short Builder or science investment is justified only if it improves the overall deadline. Settlers consume population; preserve capital productivity and allow regrowth between builds if necessary. Do not wait for a settler policy that arrives too late to accelerate expansion. Buy a Settler only if observed price, treasury, and subsequent income make it feasible.

**Builders and growth.** Allocate the initial three charges to tiles citizens can work soon: a high-value food improvement supporting growth, a productive mine supporting expansion/infrastructure, and the best remaining bottleneck. Adapt this allocation to actual yields; do not improve unworked marginal tiles merely to spend charges. Check tile-specific improvement choices. Consider an extra Builder only when its growth/production benefit repays its production and travel time before qualification. Use chopping or harvesting only with legal prerequisites and a clear timing benefit; preserve food capacity. Watch housing and amenities. Prioritize food in new cities until population 3, checking whether the focus is already enabled because `focus` toggles. Add housing only when its growth benefit warrants the delay.

**Science and economy.** Research toward Writing promptly, then choose technologies supporting observed improvements or the remaining science shortfall. Choose useful economic civics; equip available production, settler, or maintenance policies when beneficial, favoring free policy changes. Compare Campus placements and build Campuses/Libraries across productive cities while new cities grow. Use observed empire science and completion estimates to project the threshold; do not assume three Libraries automatically suffice. Account for population science, adjacency, maintenance, and lost tile yields. Build income infrastructure or use available economic policies if net gold becomes the limiting condition. Purchases must leave treasury nonnegative and affordable ongoing maintenance. Avoid optional wonders and projects that cannot support all five rounds.

**Control and verify.** Act sequentially. Use returned state, with targeted observations/choices only when needed. Verify submitted commands; never retry uncertain connection results. Resolve blockers and distinguish repeated `end_turn` requests from completed turns. Reforecast population, science, food, and gold after each completed turn; direct effort to the slowest condition. Once qualified, preserve stable yields and monitor the reported hold count each round. Finish only on explicit success or verified hold ≥5 with every condition intact; call `finish` if the trial remains open. Stop on infrastructure failure and report it factually.
